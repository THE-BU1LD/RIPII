`GREEN_NEGATIVE_LOCAL / BLOCKED_EXTERNAL_VALIDATION`

## Current evidence boundary

RIPII has real tested implementations, but no validated novelty, superiority, real-world utility, physical-renormalization claim, or external confirmation.

Corrected pilot v2 is credible local negative evidence:

- the full model reconstructs worse than the structured-removal control;
- codebooks are near-collapsed.

World-model v3 also rejects advancement:

- multiscale loses the graph OOD comparison on all five retained seeds.

## Long-range extension

A prospectively specified long-range force extension makes global information relevant but still does not validate learned hierarchy:

- multiscale fails to achieve the required consistent 5% advantage over the simple global-pool control;
- mean coupling-specific interaction is essentially zero across three seeds;
- the 300-update follow-up places global pool first in mean IID and every mean OOD regime.

## Supported conclusion

Current evidence supports simple flat/global aggregation more strongly than the proposed learned multiscale hierarchy under the tested local development settings.

## Claims not supported

- RIPII is superior or novel in a validated empirical sense;
- learned grouping is better than flat/global controls;
- local synthetic development results establish real-world utility;
- assignment activity is mechanism efficacy;
- similar parameter counts imply equal compute.
