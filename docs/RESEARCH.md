# Research overview

RIPII tests whether structured latent projections, graphs, quantization, and learned
coarse grouping improve representation learning or object-state dynamics. The strongest
defensible outcome is negative: legacy structured reconstruction fails its corrected
pilot, and learned grouping fails against a flat graph in local dynamics and against
simple global pooling when long-range coupling is introduced.

The authoritative question, hypotheses, operators, and evidence are in
`research/HYPOTHESES.md`, `research/MATHEMATICAL_SPEC.md`, `RESEARCH_TRUTH.md`, and
`EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md`. No novelty, SOTA, real-world, or population-level statistical
claim is authorized. The next scientific step requires public external dynamics data
and a compute-matched frozen protocol.
