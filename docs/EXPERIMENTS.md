- World v3: five seeds and 1,000 updates; multiscale loses graph on every paired aggregate OOD comparison.
- V3 failure localization: post-result exploratory evidence; failure is broad and largest under faster motion.

- Coupled 300-update follow-up: new seeds; global pool has the best mean IID and all mean OOD errors.
- Coupled-checkpoint efficiency profile: nine checkpoints measured under one CPU
  protocol; machine-local engineering evidence only, with incomplete profiler FLOPs.
- Legacy objective study v1: all 39 planned paired runs completed; every individual
  auxiliary and the full objective worsened reconstruction on all three seeds versus
  reconstruction+KL. Development evidence only (30 updates, synthetic data).
- Objective-gradient audit: signed three-seed artifact reports raw magnitudes, weighted
  gradient norms, and all 78 pairwise cosines. Sixty-three pairs conflict on at least
  one seed; this local diagnostic cannot establish causality.
