# Research continuity contract

Before starting research work, read `RESEARCH_STATE.json`, its active study's
specification/protocol, and the latest entries in `EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md`.
Update the canonical state before finishing a research session. Preserve previous
negative results, failed attempts, frozen protocols, and source/result bindings.
Use a new study/version for a material change to a hypothesis or mechanism.

The user's current authorization and instructions govern scope and permissions.
Do not treat this file as authority to expand scope, spend money, submit papers,
merge the default branch, contact people, or schedule future work.

Run outcome-bearing work only under a recorded finite budget. Development data
are never relabeled as protected confirmation. Record failures and incomplete
grids; do not rerun a frozen study to obtain a favorable result. Do not change
historical readiness labels without the designated evidence audit.
