# RIPII negative-result synthesis

Status: **evidence-bounded negative/development closure; no superiority, external-confirmation, or real-world claim**.

## Decision

The retained RIPII evidence does not support advancing the current hierarchy mechanisms.

This is a synthesis of already committed results. It introduces no new experiment, threshold, seed, model selection, or outcome. Historical artifacts remain authoritative for exact values.

The practical default is therefore:

- keep the simpler flat graph / simple-objective controls as reference methods;
- preserve every adverse result and failure surface;
- do not rescue-tune the current quantized, multiscale, or expanded-objective hierarchy against observed outcomes;
- require a separately frozen protocol and fresh evidence boundary for any successor claim.

## Evidence chain

### 1. Corrected frozen representation pilot

The corrected pilot-v2 full RIPII configuration is worse than the no-quantizer control on the retained local synthetic study. The evidence ledger records a mean reconstruction-MSE disadvantage of **+0.061549** versus no-VQ.

The same retained pilot reports effective-code fractions of **0.148 coarse** and **0.182 fine**, below the frozen activity/usage gate. This supports the bounded conclusion that the tested hierarchical quantizer is not justified by the retained pilot.

Pilot v1 remains historical negative evidence, but its loss-balancer defect prevents causal component ranking.

### 2. Object-dynamics world-v3 hierarchy

The stronger world-v3 development study compares learned multiscale grouping against flat graph and global-context controls under validation-selected checkpoints and paired seeds.

Retained evidence shows:

- mean IID position RMSE: graph **0.0904**, multiscale **0.0983**;
- graph has the lowest mean error on every retained world-v3 OOD split;
- multiscale mean OOD relative improvement versus graph is **-13.05%**;
- multiscale loses the graph OOD comparison on **5/5 paired seeds**;
- learned assignments remain active at **2.77 effective groups out of 4**.

The mechanism is therefore active but not useful under this benchmark. Activity alone is not evidence of predictive value.

### 3. Failure-localization and long-range follow-ups

Post-result localization reports that the multiscale deficit is not confined to contact events; the retained analysis spans contact, near-contact, forced, and free-flight subsets.

A later prospective long-range-coupling development intervention does not rescue a hierarchy-specific claim. The current ledger records a negative hierarchy result there as well, while a longer fresh-seed follow-up favors simple global pooling in the mean error comparisons.

These studies are development evidence, not external confirmation.

### 4. Objective simplification

The frozen 39-run objective study finds that no tested legacy auxiliary-objective addition improves reconstruction over the reconstruction+KL reference at the retained scale.

The full 13-term objective is worse than the simpler reference on all retained paired seeds. A post-training gradient diagnostic also finds materially unequal term magnitudes and many conflicting gradient directions. That diagnostic is mechanistic context, not causal proof.

The justified engineering/scientific direction is simplification rather than adding further auxiliary structure to rescue the observed outcome.

### 5. Repaired RIPII 0.2

The repaired 0.2 implementation is engineering-distinct from the historical studies and passes its mechanism/configuration verification, but its prospectively frozen development study still fails the advancement gate.

The evidence ledger records:

- full repaired RIPII 0.2 mean reconstruction MSE: **0.08410**;
- reconstruction+KL reference: **0.04687**;
- plain autoencoder: **0.05573**;
- both simpler controls beat the repaired full model on every paired seed in the retained five-seed study.

This is a new negative development result, not permission to reinterpret older artifacts as 0.2 evidence.

### 6. RIPII-MR

The multiresolution successor also fails its frozen synthetic development gate.

The current ledger records:

- decision: **no_advance**;
- **75** learned-model runs;
- **0/60** paired comparisons pass;
- negative mean OOD improvement against every learned control.

The implementation also currently evaluates restriction, coarse edges, and coarse impulses before route gating, so it does not support a conditional-computation or compute-saving claim.

## What is supported

The current evidence supports the following bounded statements:

1. The tested quantized/structured pilot does not justify its hierarchical quantizer.
2. Learned multiscale grouping is active in the world-v3 benchmark but does not outperform the simpler flat graph.
3. The retained long-range and external-simulator development extensions do not establish hierarchy-specific value.
4. The expanded legacy objective does not beat the simpler objective reference at the tested scale.
5. The repaired 0.2 full model and RIPII-MR successor both fail their frozen development advancement gates.
6. Across these retained development studies, simplification is better supported than post-outcome hierarchy rescue.

## What is not supported

Do **not** claim that:

- hierarchy is universally harmful;
- flat graph models are universally optimal;
- the results generalize to real physical observations;
- the studies constitute powered multi-dataset confirmation;
- the current negative result establishes novelty;
- engineering repairs reverse any historical scientific verdict;
- any successor mechanism has positive evidence before a new frozen study is executed.

The current outcome-bearing evidence remains largely synthetic/developmental, with limited seed counts and incomplete compute matching.
